map, commands, domain rules, solved pitfalls and conventions. It is imported below; do not
duplicate its content here. When behaviour documented there changes, update `AGENTS.md`,

`openspec/changes/`; the `openspec` CLI (`openspec validate <change> --strict`) checks them.
